Known bugs
==========

Spire cut in half near a courtyard portal (rm11)
------------------------------------------------
Status: open. The original Rogue Spear engine has the same bug, so fixing it is optional
(it would be an improvement over the original, not a regression fix).

What you see: in rm11's courtyard, facing south (-z) toward the roof spire, the spire is
whole when standing well inside room 5. Within 40 units of portal pt05_06, or anywhere in
room 6, the half of the spire on room 6's side of x = 32637 disappears.

Reproduce (spire cut, standing in room 6):
  cargo rr --map data/map/rm11/rm11.map --pos 32639.9,32832.0,-38155.6 --yaw -170.8 --pitch 13.2 --first-person --textured --portals
Spire whole (room 5, 80 units from the portal):
  cargo rr --map data/map/rm11/rm11.map --pos 32557.1,32832.0,-38143.8 --yaw -179.1 --pitch 21.6 --first-person --textured --portals
(--pos takes z negated; map-space z is positive. Add --no-cull to see what should draw.)

Geometry (map space):
  - Portal pt05_06 (room 5 -> 6): plane x = 32637, z 35887..41079, y 32667..34627.
    It is a wall-less opening in the open courtyard, so its on-screen mask covers the sky.
  - Spire: material shingles_snow_01.bmp, x 32307..32957, y 33677..34827, z 33797..34467.
    It belongs to room 5, and a copy belongs to room 1 (the exterior, not drawn here).
    It straddles the portal's plane about 1400 units south of the portal's end.
  - Room 5's ground volume: x 29937..34137, z 35837..40267. Room 6's volume:
    x 32095..37417, z 29317..43953. They overlap in x 32095..34137, z 35837..40267.
    The spire is outside that overlap, and outside room 5's own volume.
  Tools: examples/face-probe.rs (triangles near a point, with room and material),
  examples/room-boxes.rs (room volumes), examples/portal-list.rs.

Cause: a room drawn through a portal is clipped at the portal's plane, and the plane is
infinite (src/draw.rs, draw_plan: `fixed.push(slackened(p))`). The clip exists to remove
a neighbor's geometry that overlaps the room in front, but it also cuts the spire, which
is far to the side of the portal. In the doorway mode (within TOUCH = 40 units,
src/visibility.rs), room 5 itself is cut at the same plane wherever it is inside room 6's
floor plan box (cut_in_parent / bounds_at), and room 6's box covers the spire.

Possible fix: clip at the portal's plane only inside the box where the two rooms' volumes
overlap, and keep the room whole outside it. draw_plan already expresses "this side of the
plane OR outside the box" for touched portals by drawing the room once per way (up to 5
draws). Risks: more draws per room (measure the frame time on the big courtyard rooms), and
overlap junk lying outside its own room's volume would show. Check portals across maps
against --no-cull, and run the golden harness (scripts/golden.sh, scripts/pngdiff.py).

In the original engine (checked 2026-10-13): deeper in room 6, the spire is hidden too, but
turning left brings it back. Not replicated, on purpose. It is probably how the original works
out the screen rectangle of a portal that runs behind the camera: pt05_06 is 5200 units long,
and from here part of it is behind the player. Cut at the near plane, as visibility::project
does, the rectangle runs from the portal's far end to the edge of the screen, and the spire
is just outside it whatever the view's angle; an engine that clamps, drops or projects the
corners behind the camera gets a rectangle that changes with the angle. With --portal-rects
the spire's tip shows here, likely through room 1's copy of it.
  cargo rr --map data/map/rm11/rm11.map --pos 32900,32832.0,-38155.6 --yaw -170.8 --pitch 13.2 --first-person --textured


A room's wall covering the doorway beyond the room in front (rm11)
------------------------------------------------------------------
Status: not fixed, on purpose. The original Rogue Spear engine shows it, and so do we: the
drawing is the original's (2026-10-14). An earlier fix wiped the depth inside every opening
a room was seen through, up to 200 units past it (OVERLAP_DEPTH); it was the one rule not
taken from the original, and it was removed. --portal-paths, the older drawing, hides it.

What you see: in the doorway between rm11's hall (room 121) and room 119, looking through 119
at its far doorway into room 120 (green curtains, a star in the floor), a wood-paneled wall
stands in that doorway and covers part of the view into 120. One step into 119 it is gone,
in the original too (debug/original-engine-rm11-stairs-2.png and -3.png).

Reproduce:
  cargo rr --map data/map/rm11/rm11.map --doors-open 1 --pos 29467.9,32932.0,-39542.9 --yaw 312.8 --pitch -5.0 --first-person --textured
Hidden (one step into 119):
  cargo rr --map data/map/rm11/rm11.map --doors-open 1 --pos 29495.1,32932.0,-39551.7 --yaw -51.2 --pitch -2.9 --first-person --textured
Tests: visibility::tests::the_halls_wall_stands_inside_the_room_past_119 and
the_room_past_the_next_doorway_is_left_to_the_depth_test; golden.sh scenarios p, q, w, aa.

Geometry (map space):
  - The wall: room 121's (material wood_panel_ornate.bmp), in the plane x = 30017, around
    y 32866, z 40051. That point is inside room 120's volume (and room 1's), not 119's.
  - 119's far doorway, p120_119: plane x = 29957, z 39717..40117. The wall stands 60 units past
    it, inside room 120: the hall and 120 overlap there.

Cause: rooms overlap ("impossible geometry"). Each room in view is drawn whole within the
screen rectangle of the portals it is seen through, sorted out by depth. In the hall, the
hall is the player's room and is drawn whole, and its wall is nearer than 120's far side.
In 119, the hall is only the room behind the door, and its rectangle is the door's.

If it is ever fixed (option D in PORTALS.md): hide that wall on rm11 alone. A general rule
has to tell this wall from real surfaces seen through a room and back: by rm08's canal, the
alley's (203) far facade is seen through room 521 and back, 1000+ units past the opening,
and a depth wipe without a limit let 521 cover it with a farther building (golden.sh s).
